# Running the shop yourself — the admin panel

A guide for whoever runs Al-Madina Jewellers day to day. No technical knowledge
assumed. It is written to be printed, or read on the phone it will be used on.

The panel lives at **almadinajeweller.com/admin**. It is the same website the
customers see, with nine extra screens that only staff can open. Everything that
used to need someone to edit the database by hand — the morning's gold rate, a
new piece, a photograph — is on one of those screens.

## Signing in

Open **almadinajeweller.com/admin** and enter your email address and password.

There is no "Sign up" and no "Forgot password" link, and that is deliberate.
Accounts are made only by whoever looks after the website. It means a stranger
who finds the address cannot get in, and cannot even create an account to try.
If you are locked out, ask for the password to be reset for you — it cannot be
done from this screen.

Stay signed in on your own phone. Do not sign in on a shared or borrowed one.

## Every morning: the gold rate

This is the one that matters most. **No price on the website is stored.** Every
piece is priced from its weight against the rate you publish here, so until the
rate is set for the day, the whole site is quoting yesterday.

Open **Gold rate**, then:

1. Check the **Rate date** is today.
2. Type the **Piece (24k)** rate per tola. Type this one first.
3. The rest fill themselves in from it — Pathor (23.65k), Jewellery (22k), the
   20k rate the shop buys at, and silver. Change any of them by hand if the
   bazaar is not moving together.
4. Each row shows the per-gram figure beside the per-tola one, so you can check
   it against how you quote at the counter.
5. Publish. The **Currently live** panel at the bottom then shows exactly what
   the website is using.

The 20k buying rate is kept off the public rate board on purpose. It appears
only on the page about selling gold to the shop.

**Check the figure before publishing.** A digit typed wrong re-prices every
piece on the site within seconds. If you spot it afterwards, publish the correct
rate the same way — it overwrites, and the site follows immediately.

## Adding or changing a piece

Open **Products**. Search by name or SKU to edit something, or use **New
product**.

The fields are grouped the way you would describe a piece: **Identity** (name,
category, web address), **Specification** (net metal weight, stones, stone
weight, dimensions, ring sizes), **Pricing** and **Photographs**.

Pricing is the part worth knowing. Enter the **net metal weight** and the panel
works the metal value out from the rate you published this morning, so you are
not doing the sum twice. Making charges, stone value and any discount go in
beside it. If it says there is no published rate for that karat, set the rate
first — the piece cannot be priced against a rate that does not exist.

Up to **four photographs** per piece. Shoot them square and in daylight if you
can; the website crops to a square.

**Show in New Arrivals** is a tick box — that is what puts a piece on the front
page.

## When a piece sells

Open the piece and press **Mark as sold**. It comes off the website straight
away — out of the collections, off the front page, out of search results — and
nothing about it is lost. The weight, the making charges, the rate it was priced
against and any reviews of it all stay exactly where they were.

If it comes back, or you marked the wrong one, open it again and press **Put
back on the website**. There is no warning on either, because neither one throws
anything away.

The list at the top of **Products** shows **For sale** by default. Switch it to
**Sold** to see what has gone, or **All** to see everything; a sold piece is
greyed out and carries the date it sold. The count under the heading is how many
pieces a customer can see right now.

**Use this instead of Delete when a piece sells.** Delete is for something that
should never have been there — a duplicate, or a piece entered wrongly. It
removes the record for good.

## The other screens

**Enquiries** — the page you land on. Everyone who has written in, with a
needs-a-reply queue. Filter by bridal, product, contact or callback.

**Orders** — who has paid and who has not, who to call first, and making a pay
link for a customer.

**Custom** — requests for something made to order.

**Visits** — customers who asked to come in. Confirm or move a time, and leave a
note for whoever is at the counter.

**Reviews** — nothing a customer writes appears on the site until you publish
it. You can also mark a review as a verified purchase.

**Categories** — the groupings pieces sit under, and the order they appear in.

**Calculator** — the counter calculator. Works a price from weight and rate,
saves it under the customer's name, and prints it as a bill on the shop's
heading.

## What it will not let you do

Worth knowing before you go looking for a button that is not there.

- **Pieces cannot be dragged into a different order.** Categories can; the
  pieces inside them cannot.
- **An enquiry cannot be deleted** — only marked handled. That is on purpose, so
  a lead cannot be lost by a mis-tap.
- **A review can be deleted,** and that one is permanent. It asks first.
- **Accounts cannot be created or reset here.** See _Signing in_ above.

## On your phone

The screens are built for a phone — the nine tabs are a grid of icons, not a row
squeezed off the side of the screen.

The admin installs as an app of its own, **Al-Madina Admin**. It does not go
through Google Play, and nobody else can find it there.

1. On the phone, open **Chrome** and go to **almadinajeweller.com/admin**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to
   Home screen**). Confirm.
3. An icon appears with the rest of your apps: the gold diamond on black with
   **ADMIN** under it. The shop's own icon is the same diamond on green, so the
   two are easy to tell apart.

Open it and it goes straight to the sign-in, full screen, with no browser bar.
Sign in once and it stays signed in. **Press and hold the icon** for shortcuts
straight to **Gold rate**, **Products** and **Enquiries** — the morning's rate
is one press away.

There is nothing to update, ever. It is the website, so every change to the
panel reaches the app the next time it is opened.

Because it stays signed in, keep a screen lock on that phone.

## Is it safe?

Short answer: yes, and it does not depend on the address staying secret.

Signing in is only half of it. The website's database is the thing that decides
who may change anything, and it is set to refuse everyone except the handful of
people on the staff list. Somebody who is signed in but not on that list is
refused exactly like a stranger, and the list starts empty, so an account by
itself grants nothing.

Customer names and phone numbers cannot be read by the public at all. The admin
pages are also kept out of Google.

None of this relies on a password hidden in the website's code. There is none.
